# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Citizens reporting issues and tracking their resolutions.

## Product Purpose
JanNetra is a Governance Intelligence & Decision Support System that allows citizens to report issues and track resolutions.

## Positioning
It aggregates data from various sources and uses NLP to cluster and surface governance signals automatically.

## Operating Context
Citizens interact with a web-based portal to submit and view reports, primarily interfacing with a map-based UI to pinpoint issue locations.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Must preserve existing React/Vite frontend stack, Supabase schema, FastAPI backend integration, and the map-based UI. Integrates MapLibre and Leaflet for geospatial features. Scheduled pipeline for NLP and clustering.

## Evidence on Hand
React/Vite frontend with MapLibre, Leaflet, framer-motion, and three.js. FastAPI backend with scheduled NLP pipeline and Supabase integration.
